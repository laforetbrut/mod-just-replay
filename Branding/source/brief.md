# Just Replay: brand brief

## The project

Just Replay is a client-side NeoForge 1.21.1 mod that records Minecraft sessions and turns them into
films: replay from any angle, place and cut cameras, add actors, music, effects and texts, and
export a real video. It is used by players who make videos, server owners who showcase their
worlds, and content creators.

## Personality

Simple, clean, professional. A tool a filmmaker trusts, not a toy. It sits next to Sodium, Iris and
Distant Horizons in a modpack: a flat, confident glyph, not an illustration.

## What the logo must make someone understand

"This is where you direct your world." Recording is only the start; the mod is about framing and
directing what was recorded.

## Name and tagline

- Name: Just Replay
- Tagline: Record. Replay. Direct.

## Constraints

- Must read at 16 px (mod list, tabs, Discord) and in one color.
- Mod list logo in NeoForge: a wide box 50 GUI pixels tall, so a horizontal lockup.
- Avatars: CurseForge 400 px square, Modrinth 96 px.
- Free palette (the user did not ask to keep the current cyan and pink), flat colors only.

## To avoid

- The category clichés found below: camcorders and photo cameras, circular replay arrows, pixels
  dissolving off an arrow, a lone play triangle.
- Gradients, glow or 3D inside the mark; the Minecraft logo or font; Mojang art.

Clichés of the category: a camera or a camcorder, a circular replay arrow, pixel dissolve, red REC
dot as the main idea.

Opening: nobody draws the director's view. Framing (the viewfinder corners), the editor's own tools
(keyframes, the play head of the timeline) and the letter J are all unused. The mark stands on one
of those, as a flat glyph that holds on a solid square like Sodium's.
