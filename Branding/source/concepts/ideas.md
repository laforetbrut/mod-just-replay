# Ideas, in words, before drawing

1. A lowercase j whose dot is the play head knob of the editor's timeline and whose stem is the play
   head line crossing the track.
2. Viewfinder corners framing a block: the world, framed by a director.
3. A camera path: one smooth curve through three keyframe diamonds, the way the editor draws it.
4. A block whose silhouette is cut by a rewind chevron: the world, played back.
5. A capital J drawn as a film strip turning a corner.
6. Two overlapping frames, the second slightly turned: a second take.
7. A clapperboard whose striped top is the top face of a block.
8. A keyframe diamond holding a smaller diamond: a frame inside the frame.
9. A play head line cutting a square into two unequal parts: before and after the cut.
10. A JR monogram where the R's leg is a play triangle.
11. A circle of four arcs like a lens aperture opening on a square pixel.
12. The letter J as a crane arm holding a camera over the world.

Kept, different in kind:

- concept-1: idea 1, a letterform (the name).
- concept-2: idea 2, a symbol of what it does (framing).
- concept-3: idea 3, an abstract mark of how it feels (motion along a path).
- concept-4: idea 4, an emblem (the block, played back).
