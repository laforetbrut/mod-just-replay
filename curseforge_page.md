<p><span style="font-size:18px;color:#e5e5e5"><strong>Just Replay</strong> records your Minecraft sessions and turns them into films: replay them from any angle, place cameras, add actors and music, and export a real video.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:20px;color:#45b2f1"><em>Press F8, play, then make the movie.</em></span></p>

<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Team-Arcadia/images@main/mods/justreplay/curseforge/editor.gif" alt="The replay editor over a recording: camera list, actors, effects, audio tracks and texts, above a timeline" width="800"></p>

<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Team-Arcadia/images@main/mods/justreplay/curseforge/icons/download.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#45b2f1;font-size:18px"><strong>Video export:</strong></span> MP4 from 720p to 4K with the mixed sound, shader packs included.</p>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Team-Arcadia/images@main/mods/justreplay/curseforge/title-start-en.gif" alt="Getting Started" width="800"></h3>
<p><span style="color:#45b2f1;font-size:20px"><strong>1.</strong></span> Put the jar in the <code>mods</code> folder. For videos, install <a href="https://ffmpeg.org/download.html" style="color:#45b2f1">FFmpeg</a>.</p>
<p><span style="color:#45b2f1;font-size:20px"><strong>2.</strong></span> Play and press <code>F8</code> to start and stop a recording.</p>
<p><span style="color:#45b2f1;font-size:20px"><strong>3.</strong></span> Click <strong>Replays</strong> on the title screen and open your recording.</p>
<p><span style="color:#45b2f1;font-size:20px"><strong>4.</strong></span> Press <code>G</code> for the editor, place cameras with <code>K</code>, then <strong>Export</strong>.</p>
</div>
